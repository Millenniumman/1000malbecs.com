Description

From the top of the Andes surrounding Salta come Piattelli's Cafayate wines, grown at more than 1,795 meters of elevation. The distinctively colored mountains of northwestern Argentina, made up of many geological layers, inspired our labels and our regionally distinct approach to winemaking. The splendor of this area, with its pure water and extreme temperature ranges, which produce grapes full of flavor, make it not only one of the most beautiful but also one of the main grape-producing regions in the world.

Tasting notes & pairing

This wine represents the best of crops. An intense red color and aromas of black fruits, vanilla and smoke. These aromas are transformed to give the wine great structure and body on the palate. A soft fruit flavor and a strong, balanced finish.
