Description

NUNA In Quechua it means "spirit of the earth." And it is the name that represents our vision of agriculture for the future. Through the search for a more authentic and natural way of making wine we promote the integration between human beings, the soil and the biological world in which we participate. These wines are the fruit of our deep commitment to the vision of the vineyard as an ecosystem and the use of organic and biodynamic practices, respecting the biological cycles of the vine and the preservation of our soils, and in this way express their identity with purity. From Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo, Mendoza. Argentina.

Tasting notes & pairing

For this rosé de noir, each harvest we harvest Malbec, Tannat and Syrah early in our organic and biodynamic vineyard at Finca Nuna, Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo, Mendoza. We press, macerate and spontaneously ferment each varietal separately with its indigenous yeasts and depending on the expression of each cut we then compose this fresh assemblage that varies each vintage. Without clarification: vegan certification. Our Nuna rosé in the glass presents an irresistible and delicate Provence-style muted pink color. Its expressive floral perfume and subtle notes of berries reach the nose. In the mouth, its natural acidity is confirmed, as is its simple and fruity character. We love Nuna rosé at sunset or as an aperitif with starters, light dishes or accompanying those pending conversations that we all have.
